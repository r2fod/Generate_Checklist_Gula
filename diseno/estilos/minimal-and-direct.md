# Estilo: Minimal & Direct
> Tipo: Landing Page · Origen: 2020s Modern · Complejidad: Medium · ID: `minimal-and-direct`

- **Palabras clave**: Minimal text, white space heavy, single column layout, direct messaging, clean typography, visual-centric, fast-loading
- **Colores primarios**: Monochromatic primary, white background, single accent color for CTA, black/dark grey text
- **Colores secundarios**: Minimal secondary colors, reserved for critical CTAs only, neutral supporting elements
- **Efectos y animación**: Very subtle hover effects, minimal animations, fast page load (no heavy animations), smooth scroll
- **Ideal para**: Simple service landing pages, indie products, consulting services, micro SaaS, freelancer portfolios
- **No usar en**: Feature-heavy products, complex explanations, multi-product showcases
