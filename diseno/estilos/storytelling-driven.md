# Estilo: Storytelling-Driven
> Tipo: Landing Page · Origen: 2020s Modern · Complejidad: Medium · ID: `storytelling-driven`

- **Palabras clave**: Narrative flow, visual story progression, section transitions, consistent character/brand voice, emotional messaging, journey visualization
- **Colores primarios**: Brand primary, warm/emotional colors, varied accent colors per story section, high visual variety
- **Colores secundarios**: Story section color coding, emotional state colors (calm, excitement, success), transitional gradients
- **Efectos y animación**: Section-to-section animations, scroll-triggered reveals, character/icon animations, morphing transitions, parallax narrative
- **Ideal para**: Brand/startup stories, mission-driven products, premium/lifestyle brands, documentary-style products, educational
- **No usar en**: Technical/complex products (unless narrative-driven), traditional enterprise software
- **Modo claro**: supported
- **Modo oscuro**: supported
- **Rendimiento**: cost:moderate|drivers:animation,blur
- **Accesibilidad**: risk:low|requires:contrast-text-4.5,keyboard,visible-focus,reduced-motion
- **Móvil**: adaptable
- **Conversión**: ✓ High
- **Palabras para prompt**: Design a storytelling landing page. Use: narrative flow sections, scroll-triggered reveals, chapter-like structure, emotional imagery, brand journey visualization, founder story, mission statement, timeline progression.
- **Palabras CSS**: scroll-snap sections, Intersection Observer for reveals, parallax backgrounds, section transitions, timeline CSS, narrative typography (varied sizes), image-text alternating
- **Checklist de implementación**: ☐ Story flows naturally, ☐ Scroll reveals smooth, ☐ Sections timed well, ☐ Emotional hooks present, ☐ Mobile story readable, ☐ Skip option available
